# GrowUps — User Story, Persona Flows & 25-Module Master Mapping

## 1. System Overview & Core Philosophy
**GrowUps** is a comprehensive, AI-powered startup growth, business management, mentorship, funding readiness, learning, networking, and business-services platform.
It guides founders through the end-to-end lifecycle:
$$\text{Idea} \longrightarrow \text{Validate} \longrightarrow \text{Plan} \longrightarrow \text{Build} \longrightarrow \text{Launch} \longrightarrow \text{Acquire Customers} \longrightarrow \text{Raise Capital} \longrightarrow \text{Scale}$$

## 3. The 25 Modules & Detailed Functionalities

### 🚀 Category A: Ideation, Validation & Strategy (Modules 1–6)
* **Module 1 — Founder & Startup Profile**:
  - Founder bio, co-founders, team size, location, industry, business model (B2B/B2C/D2C).
  - Dynamic stage selector: `Idea` $\to$ `Validation` $\to$ `MVP` $\to$ `Early Traction` $\to$ `Revenue` $\to$ `Growth` $\to$ `Scale`.
  - Dynamic platform customization based on active stage.
* **Module 2 — AI Startup Advisor**:
  - Conversational AI Copilot with real-time prompt suggestions (Pricing, MVP scope, 100 first users, CAC/LTV).
  - Distinct separation between verified data vs. heuristic assumptions.
* **Module 3 — AI Idea Validator**:
  - 8-point heuristic scoring: Problem Clarity, Target Customer Segments, Existing Alternatives, Market Signals, Competitive Advantage, Monetization Viability, Operational Complexity, Validation Experiments.
  - Actionable Startup Validation Report generator.
* **Module 4 — AI Market Research**:
  - Automated TAM/SAM/SOM market sizing, customer personas, competitor radar, trend intelligence, SWOT analysis matrix.
* **Module 5 — Business Model Builder**:
  - Interactive 9-box Lean & Business Model Canvas (Value Prop, Segments, Channels, Revenue, Cost, Key Activities, Partners, Resources).
  - 1-Click AI Auto-Generate from current startup profile.
* **Module 6 — AI Business Plan Generator**:
  - 12 comprehensive sections (Executive Summary, Problem, Solution, Market, Go-to-Market, Financial Forecast, Milestones).
  - Live preview & Multi-format Export (PDF, DOCX, Shareable Link).

---

### 🛠️ Category B: Product, Workspace & Brand (Modules 7–10)
* **Module 7 — MVP & Product Builder**:
  - PRD generator, user story mapping, MoSCoW feature prioritization, tech stack recommender (Frontend, Backend, DB, Cloud), engineering timeline & cost estimator.
* **Module 8 — Startup Project Workspace**:
  - Integrated Kanban board (To Do, In Progress, Review, Done), milestone timeline, documents repo, meeting notes, AI weekly progress summaries & delay alerts.
* **Module 9 — AI Branding Studio**:
  - Brand name brainstormer, tagline generator, mission/vision positioning, dynamic logo visualizer, color palette generator with hex tokens, brand voice guidelines.
* **Module 10 — Marketing & Growth Hub**:
  - Multi-channel marketing strategies, social media content calendar, SEO keyword planner, email campaign creator, growth experiment tracker (Hypothesis $\to$ Test $\to$ Result).

---

### 💼 Category C: Revenue, CRM & Financials (Modules 11–12)
* **Module 11 — Sales CRM**:
  - Pipeline stages: `Lead` $\to$ `Qualified` $\to$ `Demo` $\to$ `Proposal` $\to$ `Negotiation` $\to$ `Won/Lost`.
  - Lead scoring, AI follow-up email drafts, meeting scheduler, activity log.
* **Module 12 — Startup Finance & Unit Economics**:
  - Revenue tracking, expense categorization, burn rate & runway calculator.
  - Core metrics: MRR, ARR, CAC, LTV, Gross Margin, CAC Payback period, interactive financial scenario modeler (Pessimistic / Base / Aggressive).

---

### 💰 Category D: Fundraising, Deck & Programs (Modules 13–16)
* **Module 13 — Funding Readiness**:
  - 20-point due-diligence checklist, investor data room file manager, cap-table simulation, use-of-funds breakdown.
* **Module 14 — AI Pitch Deck Builder**:
  - 13-slide institutional deck structure (Problem, Solution, Market, Product, Traction, Financials, Ask).
  - Live slide editor, visual slide preview, and export.
* **Module 15 — Investor Discovery**:
  - Verified directory of 500+ Angel Investors, Venture Funds, Micro-VCs, Grants with stage/sector filters and direct pitch deck submission.
* **Module 16 — Startup Programs & Cloud Credits**:
  - Direct benefit discovery: AWS Activate ($100k), Google Cloud for Startups ($200k), Microsoft Founders Hub, Stripe Atlas, OpenAI Credits, eligibility checker and application tracker.

---

### 🤝 Category E: Network, Mentorship & Ecosystem (Modules 17–20)
* **Module 17 — Mentor Marketplace**:
  - 10 mentor categories (Tech, Legal, Marketing, Fundraising, Product, HR).
  - Mentor profiles, availability slots, Razorpay booking checkout, and interactive video advisory room interface.
* **Module 18 — Business Services Marketplace**:
  - Verified service agencies (Software Dev, UI/UX, Legal/Incorporation, GST/Accounting, IP/Patents).
  - End-to-end RFQ $\to$ Quote $\to$ Razorpay Escrow Hire $\to$ Delivery flow.
* **Module 19 — Co-Founder & Talent Network**:
  - Smart skill & stage-based matching algorithm for Technical Co-founders, Growth Marketers, Product Designers, Advisors.
* **Module 20 — Startup Jobs & Internships**:
  - Curated job board for startup roles, equity-only co-founder gigs, student internships, and Founder's Office roles.

---

### 🎓 Category F: Learning, Community, Incubator & Governance (Modules 21–25)
* **Module 21 — Founder Learning Academy**:
  - 9 structured masterclass tracks, downloadable playbooks, ready-to-use legal/operating templates, interactive quizzes & verifiable certificates.
* **Module 22 — Startup Community**:
  - Live founder feed, Q&A forum, industry discussion circles, virtual Demo Day stages, founder AMA events.
* **Module 23 — Incubator & Accelerator Portal**:
  - B2B program management for accelerators: cohort intake, application scoring, mentor assignment, milestone progress dashboards.
* **Module 24 — Startup Analytics & AI Business Review**:
  - Real-time founder cockpit: unified metrics (MRR, Burn, Runway, Leads, Sprint velocity) + Automated AI weekly business audits.
* **Module 25 — Admin & Master Governance**:
  - Comprehensive controls: Startup verification, mentor KYC approval, service provider audits, Razorpay transaction ledger, AI usage telemetry, fraud monitoring, and system logs.

## 4. Module Synchronization Architecture

```
[Startup Profile (Stage/Industry)]
       │
       ├──► [AI Advisor] ──► Tailored stage prompts
       ├──► [Idea Validator] ──► Generates Validation Score & Canvas Data
       │           │
       │           └──► [Business Model Canvas] ──► [Business Plan Gen] ──► [Pitch Deck Builder]
       │
       ├──► [MVP Builder] ──► Generates User Stories ──► [Workspace Kanban Tasks]
       │
       ├──► [Sales CRM] ──► Won Deals ──► [Startup Finance (Revenue / MRR / ARR)]
       │                                            │
       │                                            └──► [Funding Readiness & Data Room]
       │                                                         │
       │                                                         └──► [Investor Discovery & Pitch]
       │
       └──► [All Operations] ──► [Startup Analytics & Admin Master Governance]
```
